# TyrePapa campaign imagery

`public/tyrepapa/tyre-hero.png` is the TyrePapa homepage hero. It is
illustrative campaign artwork, not a photograph of a catalogued tyre.

`tyre-still-life.png` and `tyre-wheel-road.png` are the two homepage story
photographs. The wheel photograph also appears in the account layout.
`tyre-open-road.png` appears on the About page. These are illustrative
campaign visuals, not photographs of catalogued tyres.
